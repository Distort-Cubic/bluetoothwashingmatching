Bluetooth device inspector

Inspect nearby BLE devices and collect the device/GATT metadata relevant to the Kiosoft Hercules app. This page does not send machine commands or change machine settings.

Scan and inspect

Tap the button and choose a device in the Android Bluetooth chooser. The browser does not allow a web page to silently enumerate every nearby device; only devices you select can be inspected.
