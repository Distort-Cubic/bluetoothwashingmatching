Nn Bluetooth inspection

This prototype discovers an Nn device, asks you to confirm its sticker number, connects, and lists accessible GATT services and characteristic properties. It does not read characteristic values, write data, enable notifications, authenticate, or control the machine.

Browser requirements

Use a compatible Chromium browser with Bluetooth enabled. Web Bluetooth requires a secure context, such as http://localhost or HTTPS. Opening this file directly may not provide a supported secure context.
